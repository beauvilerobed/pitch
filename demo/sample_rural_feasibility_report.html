Method and track record

The car count starts from FHWA road traffic in the area, adjusts the share of passing cars a wash captures for road volume, wash format, conveyor length and the population and income within 3 miles, and runs 5,000 simulations to give a likely range. It was checked against real car washes with published volumes:

82% of projections came within a third of real counts, across 11 states (23 of 28 express washes). Guessing the same number for every wash gets 57%.

When one site does a quarter more business than another, the model picks the busier one 83% of the time; road traffic alone gets 58%.

The real count fell inside the quoted range (25th to 75th percentile) for 75% of washes.

These checks cover express car washes only.

Limitations

This is a feasibility study, not an appraisal. It carries no opinion of the value of the property.

The zoning, flood, wetland, slope, road-access and storage-tank results are a desktop screen of public data, not a zoning determination, survey or Phase I environmental site assessment. Confirm them with the municipality, your engineer and an environmental professional.

Car counts are projections for an established year with a likely range, not a guarantee.

Costs, prices and loan terms in the pro forma are the inputs listed in the report. Results change if they change.
