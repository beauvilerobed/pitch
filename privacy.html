Who we are

This Privacy Policy describes how AniPoint Site Research ("AniPoint," "we," "us" or "our") collects, uses and shares information through this website and its Car Count Calculator (together, the "Services"). By using the Services, you agree to this Policy and to our Terms of Use. Questions go to rbeauvile@anipointresearch.com.

Information we collect

Information you provide. When you use the Car Count Calculator, we collect the address or map location you enter, the type of business and any optional details you add, and the estimate produced. When you use our contact form, email us or book a call, we collect the information you provide, such as your name, email address, company, phone number, site and message.

Information collected automatically. When you use the Services, we and our service providers may collect technical information such as an identifier stored in your browser, a coded form of your IP address, your device type, the referring page, and the pages you visit and buttons you click. We use this information to operate and secure the Services, prevent misuse and understand how the Services are used.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information (such as financial account or government ID numbers) through the Services.

How we use information

  • to provide, operate and improve the Services, including our estimates
  • to respond to your requests and communicate with you
  • to protect the Services, enforce our Terms of Use, and detect and prevent fraud, abuse and automated access
  • to comply with law and protect our rights

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

How we share information

We share information only as described here: with service providers that help us run the Services, when required by law or legal process, to protect the rights, property or safety of AniPoint or others, or in connection with a merger, sale or transfer of our business. Our service providers include:

  • Cloudflare Turnstile, which helps confirm that calculator visitors are people and not automated programs, and collects information about your browser and device to do so. Turnstile is subject to the Cloudflare Privacy Policy and the Cloudflare Turnstile Privacy Addendum.

These providers handle information under their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for their practices.
