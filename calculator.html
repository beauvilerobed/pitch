About this estimate

  • It's a guide for planning, not a promise. Actual traffic and sales can be higher or lower.
  • The number is a typical day once you're open and settled in. The first months are often slower while people find you.
  • "Most days" is the likely range. Busy days, slow days and bad weather can fall outside it.
  • The label shows how solid the estimate is. "Based on real car counts" is the strongest; "Based on industry data" is the least detailed.
  • The site itself matters too. Things like the exact lot, how easy it is to get in and out, how visible it is from the road, nearby competitors, prices and how the business is run all change results. A full study looks at these.
  • Don't rely on it alone to buy land, sign a lease or apply for a loan. Talk with us or your own advisor first.
  • Car washes are shown in cars a day. Other businesses are shown in customers on a weekday.
