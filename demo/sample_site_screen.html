Traffic & Demand Analysis

An established express wash at this site is projected to wash 316 cars a day, likely between 177 and 546 (25th to 75th percentile). Road traffic in the area: about 27,000 vehicles a day on the busiest counted road nearby (FHWA HPMS 2024).

Even the low end of the range clears the 150 cars a day a typical wash needs to break even.

Within 3 miles: about 50,000 residents with a household median income of about $120,000 (Census ACS 5-year) and about 24,000 people who work there (Census LODES 2024, jobs by workplace).

Competitive Mapping

5 other washes within 3 miles, 0 within 1 mile. The nearest is 2.2 miles southwest. 2 sit on a road busier than this site's. A competitor on a busier road can catch drivers before they reach you; one on a quieter road mostly serves its own neighborhood. The car count above is not reduced for nearby washes: across 33 real washes we tested, the number nearby did not predict how far a projection missed.

Sources and limitations

Road traffic: FHWA. People: Census ACS and LODES. Washes: Foursquare and OpenStreetMap. Site checks: FEMA, USFWS, USGS, EPA and town zoning maps.

The zoning, flood, wetland, slope, road-access and storage-tank results are a desktop screen of public data, not a zoning determination, survey or Phase I environmental site assessment. Confirm them with the municipality, your engineer and an environmental professional.

Car counts are projections for an established year with a likely range, not a guarantee.
